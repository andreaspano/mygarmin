## Why this shape

- Up to now almost everything has been easy. Recovery markers (resting HR 49, HRV balanced, sleep about 7.5 h) are stable, so one faster session a week is fine.
- The ride is moved to Sun 27 Sep and kept easy: gentle spinning after two run days helps recovery, while a long or hilly ride would repeat the 19–20 Sep pattern.
- The ride and the long run are well apart. Doing them back to back (50 km ride followed by the Mont Bre hill run on 19–20 Sep) was the only thing that pushed readiness down to 26.
- Recent load is already 17% above the 4-week base (408 against 348). Adding about 10% more keeps the build from turning into a spike.

## When to back off

- If readiness is under 40 on the morning of the faster session, run it easy instead.
- If the HRV weekly average drops to around 45 or lower **and** resting HR rises to 52 or more, cut the week by about 30%.
- After 2–3 build weeks, plan an easier week around 12 Oct with about 25% less volume.
